48 Hours Later Uploader

A private upload tool for the 48TH State Sports YouTube channel.

Privacy PolicyTerms of Service Our YouTube channel

48 Hours Later Uploader is used only by the owner of the 48TH State Sports YouTube channel. After the owner signs in with Google, it uploads the channel's own videos — the weekly NFL show 48 Hours Later and short clips from it — with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

This app uses YouTube API Services.
