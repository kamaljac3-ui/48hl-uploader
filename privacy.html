Privacy Policy — 48 Hours Later Uploader

Effective: September 30, 2026

48 Hours Later Uploader (the "App") is a private tool used solely by the owner of the 48TH State Sports YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos (the weekly show "48 Hours Later" and short clips from it). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth sign-in), the App uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's own channel, and reads the owner's own channel name and video list to confirm uploads.
  • The App does not access any other user's account, channel, or data.

What is stored, and where

  • The OAuth sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the App.
  • The App does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, share, or disclose any information to third parties. Data is only sent to Google / YouTube as required to upload the owner's videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, and by deleting the locally stored token.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact 48TH State Sports through the YouTube channel's About page.
